Who Can Help My Child?

Dunes International School provides a dedicated network of staff and support teams available throughout the academic year:

Safeguarding & Child Protection Lead

Contact for safety concerns, bullying reports, or immediate protection inquiries.

School Counsellor

Contact for emotional wellbeing, mental health support, stress management, and behavioral guidance.

Class Teachers & Supervisors

First point of contact for daily classroom progress, peer interactions, and academic support.

Student Anti-Bullying Leaders & Prefects

Guiding parents through awareness booths, peer mentorship, and reporting channels.
